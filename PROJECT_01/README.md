Game Title: BasketHopper

About the game: You play as a basketball and try to avoid bricks and survive. In the main menu you can choose what difficulty you want to play the game. There is easy, normal and hard. The harder the difficulty the faster the objects are and the faster your stamina goes down. You have to jump to avoid the bricks if a brick hits your ball you take 20 damage. If you hit the green box you regain 10 health and if you hit the blue box you gain stamina. If your health gets to 0 or your stamina gets to 0 you lose. 

Controls in Game
SPACEBAR: Jump
ESC: Quit Game
R: Restart

Main Menu
D: Change Difficulty In Main Menu
1: Play Game
2: Quit
